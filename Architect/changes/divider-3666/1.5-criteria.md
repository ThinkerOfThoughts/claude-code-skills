# Stage 1.5 — Criteria

**REVISED 2026-07-31** after stage-3 review. Changes: the oracle's control commit is `cf16967`,
not `d81bc0a`; the behavioural thresholds are re-derived from the corrected baseline; C15 no
longer treats file size as evidence of quality; C16 no longer blesses the degenerate `null`; the
divider-memo criterion is withdrawn with its design and replaced by a checkpoint-0 criterion; and
three criteria are added for defects the reviewers found (vacuous approval, `common.md`'s severity
model, the reviewer's declared inputs).

All criteria are **gating** unless marked advisory.

Textual criteria are checked by `oracles/check.sh`, self-tested against the frozen pre-change
files (`git show cf16967:<path>`): every CHANGE assertion must **fail** on the old file and pass
on the new one; every CARRY assertion must pass on both, and is instead self-tested by deleting
the asserted line from a scratch copy and confirming the assertion fires. **An assertion that
cannot be made to fail is a printer and its criterion is `verified = no`.**

Behavioural criteria are checked by **running Architect** — never by inspection.

## The one question both reviewers raised, and how it is resolved

Both asked whether record 3666's silence repeals record 3438's *"with their findings carried
foward"* clause, given that the same silence is being read as **non-interference** with 3438's
*"up the attempts to 4"*. Reviewer A: a reviewer who would keep the joint but has a real
observation is left with *approve and lose it* or *reject and force a re-derivation*.

**Resolved by the runner, not escalated, with grounds — and flagged upward for the owner to
overturn if he disagrees.** The two halves of 3438 are not silent in the same way. 3666 speaks
directly to *what the reviewer's instruction says* and enumerates its outputs exhaustively —
*"approve; otherwise, reject with explanation"* — so a third output channel is displaced by
positive text, not by silence. It says nothing whatever about how many attempts are made, so the
attempt count is untouched. And the owner's own worked example is precisely the case reviewer A
worries about: a true seam observation, which he ruled bought nothing at split time because the
node's next `while` pass handles it. Recorded in `decisions.md` as the runner's reading.
