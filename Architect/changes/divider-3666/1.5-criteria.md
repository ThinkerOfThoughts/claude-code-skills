# Stage 1.5 — Criteria

All criteria are **gating** unless marked advisory. Textual criteria are checked by
`oracles/check.sh`, which is self-tested against the frozen pre-change files
(`git show d81bc0a:<path>`): every assertion must **fail** on the old file and **pass** on the new
one. An assertion that passes both is a printer and its criterion is `verified = no`.
Behavioural criteria are checked by **running Architect** (stage 8), not by inspection.

## Stop-for-human triggers declared up front

- Any **blocker** at gate 4 or 7, or any **major** at stage 8.
- The run's cold reviewers disagreeing about whether 3666 repeals the four-round machinery —
  that is a scope question about the owner's words and belongs to him.
- A gating criterion that cannot be verified pre-ship. **C13–C17 are verified by the real run;
  none is deferred and none is checked by a proxy.**
