> ### FRZ-2 — the gate-7 (re-scoped set) blocker resolutions, 2026-07-29
>
> **Two criteria of this set were incoherent with each other, and the artifact could not satisfy both.**
> **N-10** required the severity model to be stated in `charter-common.md` and **"Stated in no other
> file."** **N-11** required `node.md` to restate *"a silent unilateral demotion is a violation and the
> reviewer's severity stands"* — a sentence of that model. GATE-B2 found the resulting duplication in the
> shipped set and named this the reason it would keep coming back: **a criteria set that requires breaking
> the rule it measures re-introduces the defect after every repair.**
>
> **The resolution is not to exempt the duplication. It is to split the rule along the composition rule's
> own diagnostic — *which roles can act on it?*** The prohibition (*no role may lower a severity*) binds
> every role and stays common. The **permission** (*you may contest one, and only via `Ask_human`*) is
> actable by the node alone — `redteam.md` states no contest channel exists for reviewers and
> `combiner.md` states the combiner does not demote — so by the diagnostic it was never common and belongs
> in `node.md`. **N-10 and N-11 are amended below to require exactly that.** N-11 no longer asks for a
> restatement of anything.
>
> **N-06 is amended** to state the composition rule's second clause (role → role) and to require the
> declared-duplication **register**, because the rule as written covered only common → role and the
> harness could not see either. **N-21…N-25 are added** for repairs this gate produced. **N-26 is added**
> for the negative assertion itself.
>
> Amended and added criteria are subject to cold re-review at this gate, as the FRZ path requires: a
> criterion an author edits mid-run and then measures themselves against is the self-certification failure
> this loop exists to prevent.
