# Plan — <name of what this node plans>

*The plan structure every node fills. Fill every section. If one genuinely does not apply, say so
in that section and say why — a blank section is indistinguishable from a forgotten one.*

## 1. Problem / intent
What this node plans and why. Which slice of the parent's task it owns.

## 2. Approach
How. The strategy, the key choices, the alternatives considered and why they were not taken.

## 3. Interfaces & seams
What this consumes and what it emits. The contracts to whatever sits beside it — a sibling half,
a consuming skill, a human.

## 4. Steps
The numbered, ordered plan. Each step at the granularity floor: executable by a competent
practitioner without further planning, naming the concrete thing acted on. Order is content.

## 5. Outputs & artifacts, with their locations
Every deliverable **and where it lands** — paths, filenames, directory layout. State every output
location explicitly.

## 6. Failure modes & contingencies
What can go wrong, and the fallback for each. What a step assumes and how that assumption fails.

## 7. Verification
How you would know this is done and correct. The checkable done-criteria.
