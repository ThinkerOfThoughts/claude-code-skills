# Cascade levels — planning top-down, one level at a time

A level is a tier of the decision pyramid. Start where the top sits (for example an interface the lower pieces plug into) and go down. Record the level order in the ledger as a direction line and have the owner confirm the sentence (router rule 2) before level 1; it is the only question that precedes a batch. Work one level at a time through steps a to e. The definitions (derived lines, gap rule, trio, citation check, pile-up) are in `stages/cascade-method.md`.

**a. Draft.** Write the level's design lines as derived lines. Every line cites the rule, default, ruling or owner line it derives from, with H or M. Apply stage-4's "New parts name their alternatives" rule to every line. List the items you cannot derive and the ones that are non-obvious. Where the owner may be needed, write `batch candidate: <condition>` (the gap condition or always-to-owner entry), never "ask the owner" or "open for the owner".
**b. Trio.** Run the trio for each non-obvious item. Its answer becomes a derived line.
**c. Citation check.** Run the citation check on every line of the level. Execute the routes it proposes (re-cite, trio, owner) and re-run the check on lines that changed. A line created or changed after the last check, by any later step, goes back through this step before it counts. Then assemble the candidate batch: the gap items, the review list, the H lines.
**d. Red-team.** Spawn one `stages/role-gap-redteam.md` agent (foreground). It gets the guarded-change files named in `cascade.redteam` (the charter in full; of the stage-3 file, the "Stage-3 additions" on the coverage challenge, the ratification audits and the five-lens output shape; the criteria-label audits do not apply), `stages/cascade-method.md`, the principles sheet, the ledger, `cascade.owner_transcripts`, the config's `redteam_context`, the level draft and the candidate batch. Route its findings: blocker or major, fix (re-checked as in step c) or, if it is a gap, move it to the batch, then re-run the red-team on the changed parts; minor, fix without a re-run; clean, go to step e. After two bounces on the same finding class (the cap stage 5 uses) make the item a gap item under condition (1). The red-team of a level also receives the changes made to earlier levels since their red-team.
**e. Owner batch.** Post one batch for the level, as pasted chat text in a turn with no question call. **Hard gate:** a gap item (section 1) is posted only if you name, at assembly time, its gap condition or its always-to-owner entry, with a one-line reason it needs the owner (for an always-to-owner entry, that it changes the owner's decision). Anything added to the draft or the batch after the red-team's batch audit (by a later edit, a judge, or the planner) is tested the same way when it is added. An item that cannot name one is recorded as a derived line, as the gap rule says, not posted. Layout, in this order:
1. **Gap items.** For each: the question, which gap condition or always-to-owner entry applies, options as full sentences with the current default marked, and "none of these".
2. **Review list.** The line, the rule it cites, its confidence and, for trio lines, the vote. Members: every M line, every line whose tag or citation the check changed, every derived line that differs from earlier approved text, every direction line (as a full read-back sentence), every build-time M line not yet reviewed.
3. **H lines**, verbatim, grouped by the rule they cite, for objection only.
Group sections 1 and 2 by detail: **design** (changes what the thing does for its user or what other work depends on), **mechanism** (how it is done inside), **wording** (strings, names, comments). Number the items so the owner can answer by number. An unanswered item was missed: re-surface it. Track the gap items in the Cascade block under Owner batches; the Owner questions list stays the owner's own questions.

**After the batch.** Answers become rules (`stages/cascade-method.md` "Owner answers become rules"); lines they change go back through step c. Record `batch <level> — <ts> "quote"` under Approvals when every gap item is answered and the review list reviewed. A line that changes after its level's batch was approved goes on the next batch's review list (or the build batch), and `sheet_version` is bumped as in the base stages. Stage 4 (the spec) starts once every level's batch is approved. Draft the next level once the gap items are answered; the review list can be answered later but must be done before hand-off, which needs every level's batch approved.
