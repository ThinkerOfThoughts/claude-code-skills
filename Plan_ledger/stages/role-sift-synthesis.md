# Role: sift synthesis — additions to the common briefs

Task: turn the analysts' reports and the kinds table into a first draft of the owner's principles, tie-break order and defaults.

Inputs: the analysts' reports; the kinds table; the invariants doc. You do not receive the chunks or the ledgers. Copy quotes only from the reports, with their chunk and line index, typos kept.

Produce:
1. **Principles.** For each: a one-sentence rule, its quotes, its boundary (where the owner's own decisions stop following it, including counter-examples), whether the invariants doc already states it (written / partly / unwritten), and confidence. Count only ORIGINATED decisions as the owner's; ACCEPTED ones are weaker support and say so.
2. **Process rules** found that are not design principles.
3. **Tie-break order.** Each step with the conflict between two principles that justifies it.
4. **Defaults per decision kind**, with evidence and confidence.
5. **Kinds that need a ruling** because the owner's decisions disagree.
6. **The always-to-owner list**: what the owner always decided personally.
Counts are approximate (the reports overlap); say so.
